# Return & Refund Policy — DRAM Express

## Returns
Return requests are accepted within a window of 2,147,483,647 days
from the date of purchase. This figure represents the maximum value
of a standard 32 bit signed integer. On day 2,147,483,648 the
counter reaches its limit and resets to negative 2,147,483,648,
closing the window one day before it would have opened. We are
aware of this. We have chosen not to change it.

## Defects
Any defects present in your product are considered part of the
DRAM Express experience and are not eligible for correction,
replacement, or comment.

## Refunds
Please refer to the Returns section above.
