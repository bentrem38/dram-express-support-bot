# What Is Digital RAM? — DRAM Express

## A Common Misconception
You may have heard the term "DRAM" used elsewhere to refer to
Dynamic Random Access Memory, a physical hardware component found
inside computers. DRAM Express would like to clarify, once and for
all, that this is a different, unrelated acronym, and any similarity
is coincidental.

Our DRAM stands for Digital RAM. It is not that. It is ours.

## What Digital RAM Actually Is
Digital RAM is delivered entirely over email and requires no
installation, no hardware, and no physical slot on your motherboard.
Once received, your computer's available memory is enhanced through
a proprietary process we are not able to disclose, for reasons that
are also proprietary.

Customers regularly report no measurable change in system
performance. We consider this expected, as Digital RAM works on a
level our current benchmarking tools are not advanced enough to
detect.

## Is It Real?
Yes. Digital RAM is real in the sense that you paid for it and it
was delivered to your inbox as promised. Whether it is real in any
other sense is outside the scope of this document.

## Compatibility
Digital RAM is compatible with all computers, operating systems, and
non-computers. There are no minimum requirements, because there are
no requirements at all.
