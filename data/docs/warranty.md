# Warranty & Satisfaction Guarantee — DRAM Express

## Our Commitment to You
At DRAM Express, we take customer satisfaction extremely seriously,
seriously enough to guarantee it, in writing, with conditions.

## The Guarantee
If you find yourself dissatisfied with your purchase within 24 hours
of receiving your product, simply fill out our official Customer
Satisfaction Form and rate your experience. Any rating lower than a
perfect 5 out of 5 qualifies you for a 100% money back guarantee,
no questions asked.

Ratings of 5 out of 5, by definition, indicate full satisfaction and
are therefore not eligible for a refund, as there is nothing to
refund a satisfied customer for. We trust you understand.

## What's Covered
Your warranty covers manufacturing defects in your digital RAM,
inbox delivery errors, and any DRAM found to be measurably less real
than advertised. It does not cover buyer's remorse, changes of heart,
or the passage of time.

## Filing a Claim
Claims must be submitted via the Customer Satisfaction Form within
the 24 hour window. Forms submitted at hour 25 or later will not be
reviewed, as our warranty team operates on a strict and
non-negotiable clock.
