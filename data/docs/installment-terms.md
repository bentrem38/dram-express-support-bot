## Missed Payments
DRAM Express runs a serious business. If a payment is missed, we
will not hesitate to pursue collection of assets owed, up to and
including the activation of latent sleeper protocols embedded within
your digital RAM at time of delivery. We do not make this decision
lightly. We do not make it slowly, either.

## Cancellation
Cancellation of remaining installments is technically possible and
entirely impractical. To cancel, a customer must submit a notarized
written request, in triplicate, mailed via physical post to DRAM
Express corporate headquarters, an address available only upon
written request, which itself takes 6 to 8 business weeks to
process. Upon receipt, cancellation requests are reviewed in the
order received by our Cancellations Department, currently staffed
by one part time employee working Tuesdays. Please allow an
additional 10 to 12 business weeks for a decision, which is final
and may not be appealed.
