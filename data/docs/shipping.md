# Shipping Policy — DRAM Express

## Our Speed Guarantee
At DRAM Express, we pride ourselves on speed and efficiency above
all else. Your product is guaranteed to ship directly to your
computer within 24 hours of us receiving your order, processed
within our standard 5 to 7 business week fulfillment window, which
begins only after your order has cleared our internal verification,
quality assurance, and astrological review process.

## Delivery Method
All DRAM Express products are delivered fully digitally, straight
to your email inbox. There is no box. There is no truck. There is
no tracking number, because there is nothing to track. There is
only DRAM, and your inbox, and the weeks in between.

## Shipping Exclusions
We proudly ship to every region on Earth, with two exceptions.

We do not ship to Florida. We wouldn't touch Florida with a ten
foot pole.

We also do not ship to Australia. Between the spiders, the snakes,
and whatever that thing was in the news last week, we have decided
the DRAM is simply not worth it.
